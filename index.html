About

the game

Rangolithical is a puzzle game where players will need to clear each and every room of a magical food tower in order to obtain the enchanted SUPER LEGENDAY RECIPE. Control Finn, with a fork hook, or Rita, with her giant pan, and solve a variety of puzzles through teamwork and coordenation!

Collaboration is an essential tool in order to beat the trials and tribulations that await inside this mysterious tower keep, where every piece of food has a mind of its own. Face fire spewing dragonfruit turrets, onion beetles and many delicious monstrocities in this cooperative multiplayer experience for up to two players, either locally or online!
